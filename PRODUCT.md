# Noteesta product context

## Register

product

## Users

Learners combining recordings, videos, handwritten notes, and documents into a focused Study Pill, including people looking for an open-source alternative to Turbo AI and Google NotebookLM.

## Product purpose

Build an open-source alternative to Turbo AI and Google NotebookLM, with self-hosting and model choice. Generate well-structured Markdown notes with source-supported charts and visuals from one or more selected resources. Generate roadmap, flashcards, MCQs, true-or-false, and interactive 3D alongside notes only when selected. Retrieval is scoped by the authenticated user and Pill. Email/password and Google sign-in use verified accounts and rotating JWT sessions. The public example uses bundled content, while the live workspace requires `NOTEESTA_ENABLE_WORKSPACE=true`.

## Brand personality

Calm, intuitive, friendly. Notes are the center of the experience. Turbo.ai and Notion inform the reading experience. The current frontend follows the Grove prototype's layout and color direction; Geist is an alternative prototype rather than the adopted design system.

## Anti-references

Distracting dashboards, excessive decoration, crowded reading surfaces, unnecessary setup steps, and gamification that interrupts study.

## Design principles

- Make the document easy to read and its structure easy to navigate.
- Keep source evidence accessible without losing reading position.
- Offer optional materials without obscuring notes.
- Use familiar controls and quiet, helpful feedback.
- Keep Markdown exports portable and visuals faithful to the selected sources.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Keyboard access, visible focus, semantic document structure, sufficient contrast, accessible diagram descriptions, reduced motion, responsive layouts, and text resizing. Image previews close with Escape and restore focus to their trigger.
