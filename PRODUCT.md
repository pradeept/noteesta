# Noteesta product context

## Register

product

## Users

Students combining class recordings, videos, handwritten notes, and documents into a focused Study Pill. The weekend challenge is to build for a friend.

## Product purpose

Generate well-structured Markdown notes with source-supported charts and visuals from one or more selected resources. Generate roadmap, flashcards, MCQs, and true-or-false alongside notes only when selected. Sources and retrieval remain isolated per user and Pill.

## Brand personality

Calm, intuitive, friendly. Notes are the center of the experience. Turbo.ai and Notion inform the reading experience; Geist is one requested prototype direction, not an adopted final design system.

## Anti-references

Distracting dashboards, excessive decoration, crowded reading surfaces, unnecessary setup steps, and gamification that interrupts study.

## Design principles

- Make the document easy to read and its structure easy to navigate.
- Keep source evidence accessible without losing reading position.
- Offer optional materials without obscuring notes.
- Use familiar controls and quiet, helpful feedback.
- Keep Markdown exports portable and visuals faithful to the selected sources.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Keyboard access, visible focus, semantic document structure, sufficient contrast, accessible diagram descriptions, reduced motion, responsive layouts, and text resizing. Image previews close with Escape and restore focus to their trigger.
